How it is scored

  • A score is 32 beats. Each beat is on the score if your tempo is inside the shaded band.
  • 75% or more passes the day and keeps your streak going.
  • A take starts counting on your second tap. Leaving in the middle uses it up.
  • Everyone gets the same score on the same date. The band is wide early in the week and narrower toward the weekend.
